# Valor y crecimiento

Buffett rechaza la oposición habitual entre "valor" y "crecimiento": son la misma cosa, el
crecimiento es una variable dentro del cálculo del valor.

> "the two approaches are joined at the hip: Growth is always a component in the calculation
> of value, constituting a variable whose importance can range from negligible to enormous and
> whose impact can be negative as well as positive." — 1992

Por eso considera redundante el término "value investing": invertir ya es buscar valor por
encima del precio pagado; lo contrario es especular.

> "the very term 'value investing' is redundant. What is 'investing' if it is not the act of
> seeking value at least sufficient to justify the amount paid? Consciously paying more for a
> stock than its calculated value - in the hope that it can soon be sold for a still-higher
> price - should be labeled speculation." — 1992

Y el matiz decisivo: el crecimiento solo crea valor si el capital incremental rinde bien; en
un negocio de bajo retorno, crecer destruye valor.

> "Growth benefits investors only when the business in point can invest at incremental returns
> that are enticing... In the case of a low-return business requiring incremental funds, growth
> hurts the investor." — 1992

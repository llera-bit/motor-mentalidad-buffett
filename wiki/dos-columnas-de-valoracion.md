# Las dos columnas de valoración

Buffett nunca da una cifra del valor intrínseco de Berkshire, pero sí entrega al accionista los
dos ingredientes cuantificables para que la calcule él. De los tres pilares del valor, dos se
pueden medir; el tercero, no.

> "Though Berkshire's intrinsic value cannot be precisely calculated, two of its three key
> pillars can be measured. Charlie and I rely heavily on these measurements when we make our own
> estimates of Berkshire's value." — 2010

## Primera columna: las inversiones por acción

> "The first component of value is our investments: stocks, bonds and cash equivalents. At
> yearend these totaled $158 billion at market value." — 2010

Parte de esas inversiones no está financiada con capital propio sino con el [[float]], que es
gratis mientras la suscripción no pierda dinero.

> "Insurance float - money we temporarily hold in our insurance operations that does not belong
> to us - funds $66 billion of our investments. This float is 'free' as long as insurance
> underwriting breaks even, meaning that the premiums we receive equal the losses and expenses we
> incur." — 2010

## Segunda columna: los beneficios ajenos a inversiones y seguro

> "Berkshire's second component of value is earnings that come from sources other than
> investments and insurance underwriting. These earnings are delivered by our 68 non-insurance
> companies." — 2010

Y señala el desplazamiento histórico entre las dos columnas: Berkshire empezó siendo la primera
y se ha ido convirtiendo en la segunda.

> "In Berkshire's early years, we focused on the investment side. During the past two decades,
> however, we've increasingly emphasized the development of earnings from non-insurance
> businesses, a practice that will continue." — 2010

En 2011 actualiza ambas cifras por acción y declara cuál de las dos le importa más.

> "our per-share investments in 2011 increased 4% to $98,366, and our pre-tax earnings from
> businesses other than insurance and investments increased 18% to $6,990 per share." — 2011

> "Charlie and I like to see gains in both areas, but our primary focus is on building operating
> earnings." — 2011

## El tercer pilar, el que no se mide

El que Buffett deja fuera de la tabla es la eficacia con la que el capital retenido se
reinvertirá: el factor "qué harán con el dinero" de [[valor-intrinseco]]. No es cuantificable, y
es el que más pesa.

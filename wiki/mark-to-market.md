# Mark-to-market: cuando el GAAP contamina el resultado

En [[contabilidad-enganosa]] el problema es la dirección que retuerce la norma. Aquí es el caso
inverso, y más incómodo: la norma se cumple a rajatabla y el número resultante no significa nada.

En 2017, antes de que entre en vigor, Buffett avisa de lo que hará la nueva regla que obliga a llevar
al resultado la variación no realizada de la cartera de acciones.

> "The new rule says that the net change in unrealized investment gains and losses in stocks we hold
> must be included in all net income figures we report to you. That requirement will produce some
> truly wild and capricious swings in our GAAP bottom-line." — 2017

> "Including gyrations of that magnitude in reported net income will swamp the truly important numbers
> that describe our operating performance. For analytical purposes, Berkshire's 'bottom-line' will be
> useless." — 2017

## La comprobación

En 2018 el pronóstico se cumple con precisión aritmética: cuatro trimestres que van de −25.400 M a
+18.500 M mientras los negocios subyacentes rendían con toda normalidad.

> "In the first and fourth quarters, we reported GAAP losses of $1.1 billion and $25.4 billion
> respectively. In the second and third quarters, we reported profits of $12 billion and $18.5
> billion. In complete contrast to these gyrations, the many businesses that Berkshire owns delivered
> consistent and satisfactory operating earnings in all quarters." — 2018

La causa es puramente mecánica, no económica: una cartera de 173.000 M oscila 2.000 M en un día
cualquiera, y toda esa oscilación cae al resultado.

> "our huge equity portfolio - valued at nearly $173 billion at the end of 2018 - will often
> experience one-day price fluctuations of $2 billion or more, all of which the new rule says must be
> dropped immediately to our bottom line." — 2018

## La instrucción al lector

Ante una cifra oficial que no informa, Buffett no pide que se cambie la norma: pide que se lea otra
cosa.

> "Our advice? Focus on operating earnings, paying little attention to gains or losses of any
> variety." — 2018

> "Charlie and I urge you to focus on operating earnings - which were little changed in 2019 - and to
> ignore both quarterly and annual gains or losses from investments, whether these are realized or
> unrealized." — 2019

Y aclara que ignorar la cifra no es despreciar el activo: la cartera importa mucho, solo que su valor
se cosecha de forma irregular y a su ritmo, no al del calendario contable.

> "Our advising that in no way diminishes the importance of these investments to Berkshire. Over time,
> Charlie and I expect our equity holdings - as a group - to deliver major gains, albeit in an
> unpredictable and highly irregular manner." — 2019

# Margen de seguridad

Estimar los "cupones" futuros de un negocio es propenso a error. Buffett afronta ese riesgo
de dos maneras: ceñirse a negocios que entiende y, la segunda e igual de importante, exigir
un margen de seguridad en el precio de compra. Si el valor apenas supera al precio, no compra.

> "Second, and equally important, we insist on a margin of safety in our purchase price. If we
> calculate the value of a common stock to be only slightly higher than its price, we're not
> interested in buying. We believe this margin-of-safety principle, so strongly emphasized by
> Ben Graham, to be the cornerstone of investment success." — 1992

# Float (flotante del seguro)

El float es el motor financiero de Berkshire: dinero que la aseguradora retiene pero no posee
y puede invertir mientras tanto.

> "Float is money we hold but don't own. In an insurance operation, float arises because most
> policies require that premiums be prepaid and, more importantly, because it usually takes time
> for an insurer to hear about and resolve loss claims." — 1995

Lo decisivo es su coste. Si las primas no cubren pérdidas y gastos, esa pérdida técnica es el
coste del float; el negocio solo crea valor si ese coste queda por debajo del de obtener fondos
por otra vía.

> "that loss is the cost of float. An insurance business is profitable over time if its cost of
> float is less than the cost the company would otherwise incur to obtain funds. But the business
> has a negative value if the cost of its float is higher than market rates for money." — 1995

Cuando el coste es casi nulo o negativo —como en Berkshire durante años—, el float funciona en
la práctica como fondos propios que no hay que aportar.

> "Since our float has cost us virtually nothing over the years, it has in effect served as
> equity." — 1995

> "why float wonderfully benefits a business - if it is obtained at a low cost." — 1995
